👋 About me
- 🖨️ Founder of Malachi Printing Solutions, a printing and branded merchandise business serving customers in Kampala
- 🎓 University student in cybersecurity and digital forensics, building skills in Python, Linux and networking
- 🔐 Practising on TryHackMe and PicoCTF, and exploring tools like Kali Linux, Wireshark, Autopsy and Volatility
- 💻 Web developer: I design and build client sites, from a luxury fashion storefront to a full ordering platform
- 🎨 Content creator: graphic design in Adobe Illustrator and video editing in Filmora

- 🤖 Developing solutions to real-world problems using machine learning
- 🌍 Kampala, Uganda
